24 pieces, 17 of them weird

Tangletris deals you the seven pieces you know — and then a heart, a rocket or a lopsided star. Here are all 24 shapes, how often each one turns up and where it wants to go.

Play Tangletris Chaos mode: all shapes at once

Seven classics and a tangle

It plays like the falling-block game you know until it doesn’t — think of it as a crazy Tetris. The first seven pieces are the classic tetrominoes, four cells each, and they carry the game. The other 17 are the tangle: shapes from the one-cell Dot to the eight-cell Rocket, including real pentominoes (the Plus, Cup, Zigzag, Giraffe and Puzzle) and a few no tetromino set would ever allow, like the Dice, whose cells touch only at the corners.

Most odd shapes bury a hole under themselves however carefully you place them, so a board full of them would choke within minutes. That’s why they arrive gradually, and why the hold slot matters far more here than in a classic game.

The game keeps a collection of every piece you’ve discovered — open Pieces in the game to see yours — so the rare ones are worth hunting.

Words used in the tips

Well
A one-wide column left open, usually at the edge, waiting for a long piece.
Dip
A single column one cell lower than both its neighbours.
Step
Two neighbouring columns whose heights differ by one cell.
Bump
A single column one cell higher than both its neighbours.

The tangle ramp

Every bag holds the classic seven in full — 5 Lines and 4 of each other classic. Each odd shape adds its weight in copies, but in Marathon every copy only makes it into the bag with a chance that grows with your level: 40% at level 1, 12 points more each level, and 100% from level 6. The difficulty curve is baked into the bag itself.

The classic seven

The seven tetrominoes, four cells each. Every bag holds all of them, so they stay the backbone of your stack at every level.

  • 🟦 Line

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 5 in every bag

    The only straight four. Keep a one-wide well open at the side of your stack and save it for a Tangle; lying flat, it caps a level four-wide stretch.

    About 1 in 8 pieces at level 1, 1 in 12 from level 6.

  • 🟨 Square

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    Rotating does nothing, so it’s all about where: a level two-wide floor. Dropped on a step it always buries a hole — with no flat pair of columns around, hold it.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

  • 🟪 Tee

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    The most adaptable classic: point-down it plugs a one-cell dip, flat side down it caps a level three-wide stretch, and on its side it sits on a one-cell step.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

  • 🟩 Ess

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    Flat, it wants a step that rises one cell to the right; stood up, a step that rises to the left. On a level floor it always leaves a hole — that’s a job for the hold slot.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

  • 🟥 Zee

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    The Ess in a mirror: flat, it wants a step rising one cell to the left; stood up, one rising to the right. Keep one such step on your surface and it never hurts.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

  • 🔵 Jay

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    Four ways to land: flat on a level three-wide stretch, upright on a level two-wide floor, upside down with its foot one cell lower than the bar, or long side down into a two-deep slot beside a taller column.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

  • 🟧 Ell

    • Classic
    • 4 cells
    • 4 in every bag

    The Jay’s mirror twin, with the same four landings flipped left to right. Between the two of them, almost any surface has a home for an L-shape.

    About 1 in 10 pieces at level 1, 1 in 15 from level 6.

Common odd shapes

Weight 3: up to three copies of each per bag. Small and friendly — mostly repair tools.

  • ⚫ Dot

    • Common
    • 1 cell
    • up to 3 per bag

    One cell: it fills any single hole you can reach. Thanks to the lock delay you can even slide it sideways under an overhang to plug a gap that looks sealed.

    About 1 in 34 pieces at level 1, 1 in 20 from level 6.

  • 🟢 Domino

    • Common
    • 2 cells
    • up to 3 per bag

    Two cells that never leave a hole on a level floor. Standing, it finishes a two-deep well; lying down, it closes a two-wide gap. Save it for repairs.

    About 1 in 34 pieces at level 1, 1 in 20 from level 6.

  • 🔷 Corner

    • Common
    • 3 cells
    • up to 3 per bag

    A three-cell L that sits on a one-cell step from either side and squares off any inside corner — one of the friendliest odd shapes.

    About 1 in 34 pieces at level 1, 1 in 20 from level 6.

Uncommon odd shapes

Weight 2: up to two copies of each per bag. Five- and six-cell shapes that each want a particular surface.

  • ❤️ Heart

    • Uncommon
    • 6 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    It arrives point down and needs a dip exactly one cell wide and deep; on a flat floor it leaves a hole under each lobe. Flipped lobes down, it straddles a single one-cell bump.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • ➕ Plus

    • Uncommon
    • 5 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    Rotating changes nothing, so the surface decides: it needs a one-wide dip with shoulders exactly one cell higher. Anywhere else, its arms trap two holes.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • 🏆 Cup

    • Uncommon
    • 5 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    Bottom down, it sits cleanly on a level three-wide floor and leaves a one-cell cup on top — a perfect parking spot for a Dot or the point of a Tee. Upside down, it swallows a single bump.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • 🪜 Zigzag

    • Uncommon
    • 5 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    Lying down, it fits any floor with a one-cell step in it; standing, it needs a two-cell drop between neighbouring columns. Like the Ess and Zee, it can’t land on a perfectly level floor without leaving a hole.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • 🦒 Giraffe

    • Uncommon
    • 5 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    A Line with a foot. Upright, it stands on a level two-wide floor; lying down, it caps a level four-wide stretch. It’s four cells tall, so in a deep enough well it can clear four rows at once.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • 🧩 Puzzle

    • Uncommon
    • 5 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    Messier than it looks, but forgiving: one turn plugs a one-cell dip, another a dip with lopsided shoulders, and the other two sit on a one-cell step either way. Try every turn before you reach for hold.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

  • ➡️ Arrow

    • Uncommon
    • 6 cells
    • up to 2 per bag

    Two of its turns have a flat three-wide base that sits cleanly on a level floor and leaves a staircase on top — just what the Ess, Zee and Corner like. The other two fill a staircase that climbs one cell per column.

    About 1 in 51 pieces at level 1, 1 in 30 from level 6.

Rare odd shapes

Weight 1: at most one copy per bag. The big, awkward ones — plan a spot for them or park them in hold.

  • ⚡ Lightning

    • Rare
    • 6 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Lying down, it fits a floor that steps up one cell halfway along, or a little staircase; standing, it needs a ledge two cells above the floor beside it. With none of those on your surface, hold it.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • 🌲 Tree

    • Rare
    • 6 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Trunk first: it slides into a one-wide well two cells deep, its branches resting on the shoulders either side. Sideways, it’s a four-wide bar with a knob underneath that needs a one-cell dip to sit in.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • ⭐ Star

    • Rare
    • 7 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    The meanest rare shape: whichever way you turn it, it has a pocket in its own side that nothing can drop into. Accept the hole and choose where it goes — high up, near an edge, away from your Tangle well.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • 🅰️ Letter A

    • Rare
    • 8 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Its legs straddle a one-wide spike two cells tall — exactly what a standing Domino leaves on a flat floor. Upside down, it needs a one-cell dip and leaves a two-deep slot on top for a Domino or a Line.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • 🌀 Spiral

    • Rare
    • 7 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Turn it so it opens upwards: then it sits flat on a level three-wide floor and leaves a two-deep slot in the middle for a Domino or a Line. Opening sideways, it traps two cells under its top bar.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • 🚀 Rocket

    • Rare
    • 8 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Eight cells, tied with the Letter A as the biggest piece. Nose down, it needs a one-wide dip; sideways, a two-wide dip one cell deep — on a flat floor its fins bury holes. Clearing a line with it unlocks an achievement.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

  • 🎲 Dice

    • Rare
    • 5 cells
    • at most 1 per bag

    Five cells that touch only at their corners, so it always leaves holes. Park it in hold, or drop it high up on a side where the holes won’t block a line you need.

    About 1 in 103 pieces at level 1, 1 in 59 from level 6.

Playing with odd shapes

  • Don’t flatten everything. A perfectly level stack suits the classics, but the Plus, Heart, Rocket and Tree all need a one-cell dip. Keep one somewhere away from your well.
  • Keep the hold slot for the rare ones. Holding a Square is fine; holding a Square when the Star is coming up in the preview is not.
  • Read the three previews. A rare shape is announced three pieces ahead — time enough to build the dip or step it needs.
  • Dots and Dominoes are your repair kit. Don’t spend them on open floor; save them for the single gaps the bigger shapes leave behind.
  • The Line isn’t the only Tangle piece. Anything four cells tall — the Zigzag, Giraffe, Lightning, Tree, Star, Letter A and Rocket — can clear four rows at once if the stack has the right notches, and scoring a Tangle with an odd shape is an achievement.
  • When a hole is unavoidable, choose where. Leave it near the surface and away from your well, where it’s quick to dig out — not at the bottom under ten rows.
