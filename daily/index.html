The Daily Tangle

One short game a day, the same for everyone. Three minutes on the clock, today’s pieces in today’s order, and a streak that grows every day you show up — a daily puzzle for falling-block fans.

How it works

  1. Three minutes. The clock starts with the first piece. Score as much as you can before time runs out.
  2. The same deal for everyone. The date is the seed: everybody playing Daily Tangle #N gets the same pieces in the same order, wherever they are.
  3. A fixed tangle. Each day is tangled to its own degree — between 50% and 100% of the odd shapes, in 5% steps — and it stays fixed for the whole run, however fast you level up.
  4. A new one at midnight. The Daily follows your own clock, the way Wordle does: a fresh puzzle at local midnight, with the same number for everyone.
  5. Replays allowed. Play again as often as you like — your best attempt of the day is the one that counts.
  6. Keep the streak. Play on consecutive days and your streak grows; miss a day and it starts again from one.
  7. Share it. At the end you get a short, spoiler-free result to paste anywhere, with a link that opens today’s Daily Tangle:

Tangletris Daily #12 🧩 18,430 · 37 lines · 75% tangled 🔥 5-day streak

Tips for a three-minute run

  • Check the tangle first. At 50% it plays like an early Marathon; at 100% every odd shape is in the bag, so keep the hold slot free for the rare ones.
  • Every attempt deals the same pieces in the same order. Replay, and you know when the Rocket arrives — plan for it.
  • Three minutes is short. Tangles and back-to-back bonuses pay more than playing it safe — but a topped-out board ends the run early.
  • Points scale with level. Lines cleared late in the run are worth more, and the pieces fall faster, too.

New to the odd shapes? The pieces guide shows where each one wants to go.

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Your best score of the day and your streak are kept in this browser’s local storage.

When does the next one start?

At midnight on your device’s clock — the countdown above shows how long is left.

What does “% tangled” mean?

The chance that each copy of an odd shape makes it into the bag. At 100%, every bag holds all 17 odd shapes.
